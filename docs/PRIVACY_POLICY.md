# Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 29, 2026

## Introduction

Become Chinese - Cultural Atlas ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Local Storage Only
Become Chinese is designed to work primarily offline. All your personal data, including:
- Your saved collections (cities, recipes, dynasties)
- Quiz progress and streaks
- Cultural journey completion status
- Generated Chinese names
- Recently viewed items

is stored **locally on your device** using AsyncStorage. We do not transmit this data to any server.

### Analytics
We do not use third-party analytics services. Any usage statistics are stored locally for your personal progress tracking only.

### No Account Required
You can use Become Chinese without creating an account or providing any personal information.

## How We Use Your Information

The data stored locally is used solely for:
- Tracking your cultural exploration progress
- Maintaining your collection and bookmarks
- Showing personalized recommendations based on your interests
- Calculating quiz streaks and achievements

## Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transfer any of your data to third parties. All data remains on your device.

## Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, its security depends on your device's security settings. We recommend keeping your device locked with a passcode or biometric authentication.

## Children's Privacy

Become Chinese is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app contains educational content about Chinese culture, history, and traditions.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy in the app.
