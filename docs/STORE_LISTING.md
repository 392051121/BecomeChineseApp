## Full Description

Become Chinese is a cultural atlas for anyone curious about Chinese culture.

Explore the rich tapestry of China through:

- Cities: Discover the character and stories of Chinese cities
- Cuisine: Learn the cultural significance of regional dishes
- Dynasties: Journey through 4,000 years of history
- Seasons: Follow the 24 Solar Terms with daily rituals

Designed for English-speaking users who want to understand China beyond surface-level tourism. Each piece of content connects to related topics, helping you build a personal cultural map.

Features:
- Daily seasonal rituals aligned with traditional calendar
- Cultural context that explains why it matters
- Beginner-friendly explanations for every topic
- Personal collection and progress tracking
- Beautiful share cards to celebrate your discoveries
- Explore themed cultural journeys like the Silk Road, Tang Poetry Trail, and Ancient Capitals
- Track your progress across provinces and dynasties

Start your cultural journey today.
