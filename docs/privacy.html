本应用完全离线运行，不上传、不收集任何个人信息。
一、我们不收集任何数据

1. We Collect Nothing

Become Chinese is a fully offline, local-only application. All content — solar terms, travel, food, history, collections, and your learning progress — is stored entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information.

二、无账户、无追踪

2. No Accounts, No Tracking

There is no account system, no login, no registration, and no user profiling.

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No advertising or third-party ad networks.
  • No tracking cookies, identifiers, or fingerprinting.
三、无需联网

3. No Network Usage

The app does not require an internet connection and makes no network requests. The only network capability is a local connectivity check, which never leaves your device.

四、权限与设备数据

4. Permissions & Device Data

We do not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photos. No device identifiers are collected or transmitted. Your data stays on your device.

五、本地存储

5. Local Storage

Your favorites, badges, name collection, and progress are saved on your device only. You can clear this data at any time by deleting the app or clearing app storage. We have no way to access this data remotely.

六、儿童隐私

6. Children's Privacy

This app is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, including children, there is nothing to be collected or shared.

七、政策变更

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will appear in-app here and the effective date above will be revised.

八、联系我们

8. Contact

Since we collect no data, there is nothing to request, access, or delete. For any questions about this policy, you may contact us through the app store listing, or email us at 392051121@qq.com.
