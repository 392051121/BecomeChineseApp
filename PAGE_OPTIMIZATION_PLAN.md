# BecomeChineseApp Page Optimization Plan

This document turns the product and development direction into a page-by-page optimization guide. It is intended to keep the app aligned with mature product patterns inspired by apps such as Xiaohongshu, WeChat, and Alipay, while staying true to BecomeChineseApp’s cultural atlas identity.

---

## 1. Optimization Goals

The app should feel:
- clear
- modern
- readable
- culturally distinctive
- English-first
- rewarding to use repeatedly

The experience should make it easy for a user to:
- know what to do next
- understand what each page is for
- see progress after every meaningful action
- continue exploring through related paths
- feel that cultural knowledge accumulates into a personal atlas

---

## 2. Universal Page Rules

These rules apply to every screen.

### 2.1 One primary action per screen
Each page should have one dominant action that is visually and behaviorally obvious.

### 2.2 Content first
The content should come before decoration. Visual styling must support comprehension.

### 2.3 Consistent feedback
Every meaningful interaction should show visible feedback, such as:
- saved state
- selected state
- stamp feedback
- progress update
- haptic pulse

### 2.4 Related paths must be actionable
Related content should not feel like a passive suggestion. It should help the user continue the atlas journey.

### 2.5 English-first readability
English should lead the page. Chinese should support understanding, not block it.

### 2.6 Calm visual hierarchy
Use clear spacing, restrained color, and stable card styling so the app feels polished rather than busy.

---

## 3. Home Page Optimization

### 3.1 Page role
Decision layer, entry point, and daily action launcher.

### 3.2 Main objective
When the user opens Home, they should immediately know what the most important next step is.

### 3.3 Optimization tasks
- keep one strongest daily CTA
- make the Today’s Ritual card the visual anchor
- reduce the visual weight of secondary module shortcuts
- keep progress metrics compact and easy to scan
- surface the user’s recent cultural path or latest save
- keep homepage copy short and direct

### 3.4 Success criteria
- user knows what to do within one glance
- the page does not feel crowded
- secondary entries remain useful but do not compete with the main action

---

## 4. Seasons Page Optimization

### 4.1 Page role
Daily ritual page and answer feedback page.

### 4.2 Main objective
Make the daily task feel complete, visible, and rewarding.

### 4.3 Optimization tasks
- keep the date and seasonal context readable
- maintain one daily question or ritual flow
- make solved and unsolved states obvious
- keep streak and solved totals visible
- strengthen stamp feedback and completion state
- keep answer options consistent in size and behavior
- keep explanations short, useful, and culturally informative

### 4.4 Success criteria
- the user can complete the daily action quickly
- completion feels meaningful
- the page feels like a ritual, not a long article

---

## 5. History Page Optimization

### 5.1 Page role
Civilizational storytelling and dynasty exploration.

### 5.2 Main objective
Make history feel like an editorial chapter sequence rather than a reference table.

### 5.3 Optimization tasks
- keep dynasty cards visually strong and chapter-like
- control the amount of emperor detail shown at once
- use expandable sections for deeper reading
- preserve clear related paths to food and places
- make chapter hierarchy and card hierarchy consistent
- reduce the feeling of chronological clutter

### 5.4 Success criteria
- the page feels curated
- the user can quickly identify the main story
- the next exploration step is always visible

---

## 6. Food Page Optimization

### 6.1 Page role
Regional cuisine storytelling and cultural tasting.

### 6.2 Main objective
Make food feel like a regional story, not a recipe database.

### 6.3 Optimization tasks
- keep dish imagery visually inviting
- standardize the card structure for each dish
- emphasize province association and cultural story
- show taste, etiquette, and substitution notes clearly
- keep related paths visible and actionable
- make collection feedback prominent and meaningful

### 6.4 Success criteria
- the user understands that the dish belongs to a place and a culture
- the page is attractive without becoming decorative
- related exploration feels natural

---

## 7. Places Page Optimization

### 7.1 Page role
City reading and regional character exploration.

### 7.2 Main objective
Make cities feel like essays about lived place, not points on a map.

### 7.3 Optimization tasks
- reduce the visual dominance of the geographic entry block
- make city cards the main browsing flow
- present city character, local life, and highlights in a clear hierarchy
- keep expanded content easy to scan
- keep related paths to food and history obvious
- preserve the feeling of regional texture without overwhelming the user

### 7.4 Success criteria
- the page feels like a city story collection
- map elements support rather than dominate
- browsing remains smooth and readable

---

## 8. Persona Page Optimization

### 8.1 Page role
Identity archive, progress summary, and final cultural overview.

### 8.2 Main objective
Make Persona feel like the final summary of the user’s cultural journey.

### 8.3 Optimization tasks
- present the generated or saved identity clearly
- show streak, solved total, badges, saved names, and connected provinces
- make the atlas map readable and meaningful
- keep milestone content compact and organized
- make the share card polished and complete
- keep the page calm, reflective, and archive-like

### 8.4 Success criteria
- the user feels that progress accumulated into an identity
- the page feels like a destination rather than a settings page
- sharing becomes appealing

---

## 9. Shared System Optimization

### 9.1 Visual system
Unify:
- background tone
- card radius
- border strength
- shadow usage
- spacing scale
- typography hierarchy
- accent color usage

### 9.2 Interaction feedback system
Unify:
- saved state
- selected state
- solved state
- stamp feedback
- haptic response
- progress updates

### 9.3 Empty and loading states
Every page should have a calm and understandable response for:
- loading
- no saved content
- unsolved daily tasks
- no related paths yet

### 9.4 Content structure system
Standardize:
- title
- subtitle or summary
- supporting note
- action area
- related paths

---

## 10. Recommended Implementation Order

### Phase 1
- unify the visual system
- tighten the Home page
- strengthen Seasons

### Phase 2
- standardize related paths
- strengthen Persona
- refine shared feedback and state patterns

### Phase 3
- refine History
- refine Food
- refine Places

### Phase 4
- polish copy
- polish spacing and hierarchy
- validate on device

---

## 11. Priority Summary

If implementation time is limited, focus on these five items first:
1. unify the visual system
2. tighten Home
3. strengthen Seasons
4. standardize related paths
5. strengthen Persona

These changes deliver the biggest improvement in product clarity, maturity, and repeated use.

---

## 12. Final Product Standard

The app is successful when it feels like:
- a modern editorial culture product
- a personal atlas that accumulates meaning over time
- a clear, rewarding, and readable experience for international users
- a culturally distinctive product without visual clutter
